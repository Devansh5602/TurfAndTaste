# Turf & Taste Domain Skill Reference

## Core Directives:
1. **Single Property:** Turf & Taste is one multi-facility sports & dining property located in **Patan, Gujarat**.
2. **Hierarchy:** Property -> Section / Category -> Physical Facility -> Service & Add-On.
3. **Sports Setup:**
   - Box Cricket: 2 physical turfs (`Turf 1`, `Turf 2`).
   - Pickleball: 2 physical courts (`Court 1`, `Court 2`).
   - Skating: 1 physical rink.
   - Cricket Green Nets: 1 physical practice net area.
   - Ball-Shooting Machine: Paid optional add-on on the Cricket Green Net area (NOT a separate physical court).
4. **24/7 Operations:** Sports facilities operate 24/7 conceptually by default.
